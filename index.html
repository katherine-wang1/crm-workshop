1Anatomy of a skill

Name and description decide when Claude uses the skill. The body decides how. Tap a part on the right to see where it is in the skill.

Loading crm-company…

2Open the zip

Your crm-company.zip holds a folder with two files:

SKILL.md
The method

The same for everyone. How to add, research and update a company, and the rules it follows.

crm-config.txt
The destination

Yours. Which Airtable base to write to.

Base URL: paste your Base URL in Step 3
Base ID: …
Time zone: America/Los_Angeles
Schema version: 1

That's why this website generates the config and never touches SKILL.md: everyone gets the same method, and only the destination changes.

There's no single right structure. A few ways it could have gone:

  • Facts about you moved to memory. We started with a my-profile.txt file in the zip, and moved it to Claude's memory. Who you are isn't specific to one skill.
  • Config could live inside SKILL.md. Fine for a skill only you use. We split it out so one SKILL.md works for everyone.
  • You could add reference files, like a schema file or example outputs, for Claude to read when it needs them.
  • You could share one config across several skills.

The skill you build in Step 7 may end up structured differently. That's the point.

6Part B · Capture a conversationIn sessionCheckpoint

Capture a conversation: crm-capture-convo

But wait…

We've been updating the CRM without any human in the loop. What if we want more control?

Capture is where it matters: it can overwrite information. Maya's Next action currently says you're meeting her for coffee (from Step 5). Capturing the transcript replaces it with this conversation's action items. AI also wrote those from a transcript, so a misread commitment would land in your CRM.

So this skill shows you a preview first. It shows what's being replaced and dropped, and nothing is saved until you confirm:

People › Maya Chen
Status: Conversation scheduled → Conversation held
Last contact: 2026-10-05
Next action: Coffee with Maya on 2026-10-05 → 1) Send Maya your resume and a few lines on what you'd want to work on; 2) Waiting on Maya: intro to Priya, by Friday
Dropped from Next action: Coffee with Maya on 2026-10-05
…
NOT SAVED YET. CONFIRM?

Here's the Safety rule that creates the preview. crm-company and crm-person have a different rule 5 ("Save directly").

5. Preview, approve, write, verify. Before any write, show what will be saved and wait for my approval. Write only what was approved.
